# Decisions and sources

## Direction
- Concept: hybrid of Luminescence (hero sample, spectrum for research) and Workbench (every project as a realistic object). Chosen by Cristiano over a terminal/stage concept.
- Hosting: GitHub Pages user site, `WhySoSeriusBro.github.io`. Two languages, PT and EN, with a switch.
- Audience: recruiters, industry and academia at the same time. Everything may be disclosed except what `content/site.json → theatre.doNotList` and the privacy rules exclude.

## Inputs from other models (Nov 2026)
- Perplexity: Astro with islands is the right fit for a static, content-first site with one heavy interaction; keep WebGL as progressive enhancement; mobile fallback; performance budgets; `Person` and `ScholarlyArticle` structured data.
- Gemini (visual critique of the first previews): keep text legible over particles, persistent top bar with CV, horizontal project rows instead of angled spectrum labels, a rich case-study panel, touch-first pulses, reduced motion, brighter violet line.
- ChatGPT: palette (graphite, electric cyan, instrument amber), tagline "Making the invisible visible." / "Tornar o invisível visível.", spectrum for research and bench for experiments. Its project copy was not adopted as is: it invented results, called the degree an integrated master's, and used pre-1990 spelling.
